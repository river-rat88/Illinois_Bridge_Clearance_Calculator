The Corps chart's 440.3-ft flat pool and the LSLI2 430.00-ft zero plus 10.20-ft flat-pool stage from the station listing differ by 0.10 ft. This comparison is a research check, not a verified datum or hydraulic tie. The owner's 505.8 NAVD88 and Appendix B's 506.6 NGVD29 have a numerical difference of **−0.8 ft**. The owner's reported NGS shift of **−0.12 ft** applies to the *gauge location*, not automatically the bridge. If −0.12 were provisionally applied to Appendix B low steel, it would give **506.48 ft NAVD88**, **0.68 ft higher** than the owner's chart value. This conditional arithmetic demonstrates that datum labels alone do not reconcile the sources; do not treat either difference as a measured bridge datum shift. Check the source page, span and survey basis. The hydraulic-model chord is yet another distinct figure.

An older NGVD29 gauge zero can still define present-day **stage**. Stage is measured relative to the local zero, and changing the geodetic elevation assigned to that zero does not itself change the stage observation. To compare a water elevation to a NAVD88 bridge elevation, a correctly scoped gauge-zero NAVD88 tie must be added to stage. [NGS's sign convention](https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/VERTCON3/sign-convention.shtml) is `NAVD88 = NGVD29 + model shift`. NGS's [NCAT/VERTCON tool](https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/VERTCON3/) models a location-specific shift; a result should include input coordinates, transformation version and local error. The zero's station epoch/physical benchmark tie also matters. An NGS model output is not by itself a survey of the bridge-to-gauge water-level difference.

## Implemented feed and display

`/api/lincoln` archives the raw NOAA [gauge metadata](https://api.water.noaa.gov/nwps/v1/gauges/lsli2), [observed stage](https://api.water.noaa.gov/nwps/v1/gauges/lsli2/stageflow/observed), and [forecast](https://api.water.noaa.gov/nwps/v1/gauges/lsli2/stageflow/forecast) with hashes, timestamps and a replayable receipt. It checks station/product identity, gauge zero, units, observation order and revisions, issue/generation time, range, source integrity, and forecast run/coverage. Stage is in feet **above the NGVD29 gauge zero**. The strict late threshold is age greater than 24 hours. A failed refresh may show the last accepted stage as historical only.

Forecast direction is calculated from a complete, labeled 24-hour station forecast window, with a 0.1-ft deadband. It describes **LSLI2 only**; the association is not approved. A missing or stale forecast does not alter stage status. The clearance remains `NAVD88_REFERENCE_REQUIRED` with null value regardless of a valid stage and forecast. This is intentional, not a zero-clearance indication. The older 66.3-ft Corps research value is not shown as the selected listed clearance. The owner-reported 505.8-ft NAVD88 low steel and 429.88-ft NAVD88 converter result are carried in the audit reference, but neither is used to calculate clearance yet.

The NOAA feed does not supply a per-reading approval/quality flag in this response. An accepted API reading is labeled as an unverified point-quality observation; external quality validation remains open. Bounds 0–45 ft suppress nonsensical stage values in this pilot, not a claim about the range of physically possible floods. `var/lincoln/` stores the local immutable snapshots; `LINCOLN_DATA_DIR` overrides that path.
