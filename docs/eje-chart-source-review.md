# EJE chart correction source review

Reviewed September 27, 2026. The EJE pilot records the owner's report that the chart gives a **−0.21 ft NGVD29-to-NAVD88 correction**. The owner confirmed the direction: add −0.21 ft to Dresden NGVD29 tailwater. This review seeks a dated, identifiable chart source for that exact local correction. It does not change the pilot formula or claim that the six-inch accuracy target is met.

| Source inspected | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| [USACE Dresden station information](https://rivergages.mvr.usace.army.mil/WaterControl/stationinfo2.cfm?dt=S&fid=&sid=IL04) and [USACE water-data location](https://water.usace.army.mil/overview/mvr/locations/il04) | Dresden IL04 is identified as NGVD29; the Corps station description places its gauge upstream of the EJE bridge. | Neither page gives the reported −0.21 ft local transform, its effective date, or a surveyed bridge-water tie. |
| [USACE 2013 Illinois Waterway chart index](https://www.mvr.usace.army.mil/Missions/Navigation/Navigation-Charts/Illinois-Waterway/) and [Chart 91](https://www.mvr.usace.army.mil/Portals/48/docs/Nav/NavigationCharts/ILW/CHART_91.pdf) | The public paper chart is labeled **2013**, Chart 91, miles **268.8–271.8**, which includes EJE mile 270.6. | This is not proof of which electronic chart or update is installed on the owner's vessel; the displayed chart material did not identify the −0.21 ft correction. |
| [Official IENC S-57 cell index](https://ienccloud.us/ienc_s57.html) | The public Illinois cell **U37IL257** spans miles **257–285**. At review, its table showed edition/update **45.2** and **cleared-through date September 2, 2026**. | A public current cell listing cannot establish the edition loaded in the owner's ECS or that the particular bridge popup and correction came from that edition. The zipped feature data was not examined. |
| [IENC GeoPDF index](https://ienccloud.us/ienc_geopdf.html) and [U37IL257 GeoPDF](https://ienccloud.us/ienc/products/files/u37/geopdf/U37IL257_GeoPDF.pdf) | A separate GeoPDF product covers miles 257–285; its index listed edition 43.0, cleared through June 18, 2024. | This is a different product/version from the current S-57 listing; extracted map text did not include the −0.21 ft bridge correction. Do not substitute this edition for the owner's ECS edition. |

**Result:** The source datum NGVD29 and the relevant public chart coverage are corroborated. The **−0.21 ft amount, its local scope, chart product/edition and survey-effective date are still owner-reported rather than independently documented**. The EJE reference correctly retains `chartProduct`, `chartEdition`, `surveyEffectiveDate` and `unitRealization` as `null`, and `independentlyVerified: false`. Pilot receipts preserve that status; production eligibility remains false.

To close this specific evidence gap, obtain a screenshot or export of the EJE bridge feature showing the −0.21 ft note and its complete datum-direction wording, plus the ECS chart information panel showing the cell/product identifier, edition, update and source date. If available, a Corps benchmark or survey tie for the same reach would independently test the transform and its epoch. Do not relabel the public 2013 chart or the current U37IL257 cell as the owner's chart until those identifiers match.
