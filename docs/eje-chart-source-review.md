# EJE bridge and datum source review

Reviewed September 27, 2026. The owner clarified that the e-chart supplies the bridge clearances and low-steel elevations in **NAVD88**. The **−0.21 ft** note comes from the NOAA Dresden tailwater hydrograph, not the chart. The owner confirmed the intended pilot direction: add −0.21 ft to USACE Dresden NGVD29 tailwater before subtracting from EJE's 543.5-ft fully open low steel NAVD88. This does not establish that the cross-agency conversion meets the six-inch accuracy objective.

**Pilot formula:** `clearance ft = 543.5 NAVD88 − (USACE IL04 tailwater ft NGVD29 + NOAA CDII2 NAVD88 zero −0.21 ft)`. The −0.21 value belongs to NOAA gauge metadata; its application to the Corps series is a scoped pilot assumption. A 482.71-ft Corps tailwater would produce 482.50-ft estimated NAVD88 water and 61.0-ft clearance. The 2-inch bridge/gauge assumption is recorded, not deducted. The fully open position is assumed, not monitored.

The reference keeps `effectiveEpochVerified`, `crossAgencyGaugeTieValidated`, `independentlyVerified`, `totalErrorValidated` and `productionEligible` false. The live adapter withholds clearance if NOAA metadata is missing, changes identity/location/datum, or fails its snapshot checks. NOAA's CDII2 forecast endpoint had no forecast points at review; the EJE forecast direction remains unavailable. A [frozen same-timestamp comparison](eje-feed-reconciliation.md) records the owner's 0.07-ft observation and six larger-than-2-in differences among 89 source pairs.
