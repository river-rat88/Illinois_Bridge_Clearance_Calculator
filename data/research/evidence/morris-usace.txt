Location of Gage :
Located in Grundy County, at the south edge of Morris, Il. on the right bank .3 miles downstream of the State Route 47 bridge and .3 miles upstream from Nettle Creek at river mile 263.1.
The National Weather Service information is also linked in the Additional Links for this station.
